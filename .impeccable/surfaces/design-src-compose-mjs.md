Scope: morning screens and ambient pieces for the bathroom e-ink panel. Mode: Operate (glanceable instrument); the ambient pieces are Experience.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The morning as a Braun instrument. One screen, composed fresh each day from a fixed set of parts. It refuses the dashboard of identical tiles.
OWN-WORLD: White housing, #393039 grotesk type (Archivo), 1px rules, a 12-column grid. The data is drawn as speaker-grille perforation. Colour only as Braun control dots: orange = act, red = now, blue = water, yellow = sun, green = done.
STORY: The owner glances once and knows the weather's shape, the next thing, when to leave, and whether to water the plants. Some mornings the composition itself makes them stop.
FIRST VIEWPORT: The whole panel. A dateline, a 1px rule, then the template's lead area (dial scale, poster words, weather field or timeline band) at hero scale, with small modules underneath or in the rail.
FORM: Brief-pinned direction (Braun/Rams), with the grille as the signature move. seed: none (brief-pinned; concept-seed not run).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Unresolved: the canvas SVG sanitiser; whether yellow reads on the real panel; the real panel's white point.
